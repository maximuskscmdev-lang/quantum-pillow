The science behind the pillow

Why the materials behave the way they do — the short version.

❄

Cooling that actually pulls heat away

Specialized cooling fills such as bamboo charcoal and graphene conduct heat away from the body multiple times faster than copper. That pulls core temperature down through the night, which is what stops night sweats.

Thermal regulation
◠

Slow rebound, steady support

Built on bioclay or memory foam, the fill has a slow-rebound property traditional pillows do not. It absorbs the shape of your head and neck instead of pushing back, then recovers slowly — which is what keeps the spine aligned rather than bouncing you around through the night.
